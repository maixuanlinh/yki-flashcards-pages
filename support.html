3000 Finnish Words support

Contact support@essaykingapp.com

Include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. A screenshot can help, but remove private information first. Never send passwords or payment card details.

What can I use for free?

Basic English–Finnish cards, word pronunciation and reminders are available across all 3,000 words. The 211-word A set includes complete lessons for free. Premium is an optional one-time purchase unlocking all complete illustrated lessons, examples, translations and automatic study playback. There is no subscription.

How do I buy or restore Premium?

Open Progress and the Premium screen. The purchase button shows the price supplied by the App Store for your region. To restore a previous purchase, use Restore Purchases with the same purchasing Apple Account. Learning history stays in local app storage and is not restored by restoring Premium. Purchases on Android are separate.

The price is not loading

Check your internet connection and that the App Store is available, then choose “Try loading price again.” The free cards remain available. If the issue continues, send support your app and iOS versions. A purchase cannot complete until the App Store supplies the product and price.

How do reminders work?

Configure word reminders from Progress and allow notifications when prompted. Check iOS Settings if they do not appear; Focus and notification settings can affect delivery. Reminders do not require a server account.

Why is pronunciation silent?

Check device volume and audio output, and that a voice for the requested language is available in device settings. Automatic study runs in the foreground and pauses when the phone locks or the app leaves the foreground.

Can I move my progress?

Progress is stored locally in this app. Keep a device backup before replacing your phone or deleting the app. Artwork exports transfer artwork, prompts and memory cues; they are not a full learning-history backup. Reinstalling can remove local learning history.

Refund requests

Apple processes iOS purchases and refund requests. Visit Apple’s purchase support to request help with a charge.

About the app

3000 Finnish Words is an independent Finnish vocabulary learning app from Linapps. It is not affiliated with the YKI exam organiser and does not promise an exam result.
